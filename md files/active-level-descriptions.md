# British Swim School — Active Level Descriptions

A portable, production-ready curriculum reference detailing every active British Swim School class level across all age tracks, ---

\---

1. ## &#x20;Under 3 Years Old Track (Infants \& Toddlers)

### Tadpole

* **Target Age**: 3 months to 36 months (Beginner / Water Acclimation)
* **Ratio**: 6:1 (Parent in the water with child)
* **Level Color**: `#FFFFE0` (Light Cream / Yellow)
* **Core Goal**: Water acclimation to reduce fear, build trust, and prepare for independent back floating and safe pool entry.
* **Sales \& Outreach Script**:

> \\\\\\\*"This is the 1st of 3 survival levels where we start to build the independent backfloat and gentle jump into the pool so babies and toddlers learn to save themselves if they ever fall into water."\\\\\\\*

\---

### Swimboree

* **Target Age**: 3 months to 36 months (Intermediate Survival \& Separation)
* **Ratio**: 4:1 (Parent in water, transitioning out)
* **Level Color**: `#00C992` (Mint Green)
* **Core Goal**: Building independence from parent, one-on-one rapport with the instructor, and rolling from face-in to back float.
* **Sales \& Outreach Script**:

> \\\\\\\*"This is the 2nd of 3 survival levels where we continue building the independent backfloat and jump into the pool, but also start working on rolling over if they fall face-first into the water. We also transition students to work directly with our instructors so parents can step out of the water in the next level."\\\\\\\*

\---

### Seahorse

* **Target Age**: Under 3 years old (Advanced Toddler Survival / Independent)
* **Ratio**: 4:1 (Parent is **out of the water**, sitting on deck)
* **Level Color**: `#C7008C` (Deep Magenta)
* **Core Goal**: Full independent survival sequencing: jump into deep water, rollover to back, float for 20 seconds, and climb out unassisted.
* **Sales \& Outreach Script**:

> \\\\\\\*"This is the 3rd of 3 survival levels where we combine the first two levels' skills into one full movement: jumping into the pool, rolling over to float, then swimming or climbing out to safety. This is a much smaller class size (4:1) with the parent out of the water. Once they turn 3 or master these skills, they graduate to Starfish or Minnow."\\\\\\\*

## 2\.  . Kids 3–12 Years Old Track (Over 3)

### Starfish

* **Target Age**: 3 to 12 years old (Beginner / Fearful or Hesitant)
* **Ratio**: 4:1 (Parent on deck)
* **Level Color**: `#FF9900` (Orange)
* **Core Goal**: Water acclimation, conquering fear/hesitation, establishing breath control, and basic back floating.
* **Sales \& Outreach Script**:

> \\\\\\\*"This is the 1st of 3 survival levels for kids over 3 where we conquer water hesitation, teach relaxed breath control, and build the independent backfloat so your child has the foundation to stay safe around water."\\\\\\\*

\---

### Minnow

* **Target Age**: 3 to 12 years old (Elementary Survival \& Propulsion)
* **Ratio**: 4:1 (Parent on deck)
* **Level Color**: `#FFFF00` (Bright Yellow)
* **Core Goal**: Safe water entry, independent gliding, rolling from stomach to back, and 20-second independent back float.
* **Sales \& Outreach Script**:

> \\\\\\\*"This is the 2nd of 3 survival levels where we lock in the 20-second independent backfloat, teach unassisted rollovers when submerged, and introduce flutter kicks and elementary backstroke to move through the water."\\\\\\\*

\---

### Turtle 1

* **Target Age**: 3 to 12 years old (Intermediate Survival \& "Safer Swimmer" Benchmark)
* **Ratio**: 4:1 (Parent on deck)
* **Level Color**: `#FF0022` (Red)
* **Core Goal**: Combining survival sequencing with continuous propulsion across the pool to earn official **"Safer Swimmer"** certification.
* **Sales \& Outreach Script**:

> \\\\\\\*"This is the 3rd of 3 survival levels where we combine all survival skills into one continuous sequence: jump in, roll onto the back, float for 20+ seconds, and swim to safety using elementary strokes and freestyle. Mastering this makes your child a certified 'Safer Swimmer' ready for stroke development."\\\\\\\*

\---

### Turtle 2

* **Target Age**: 3 to 12 years old (Stroke Development 1 — Freestyle \& Backstroke Mastery)
* **Ratio**: 6:1
* **Level Color**: `#99FF00` (Lime Green)
* **Core Goal**: Transition from survival to technical swimming; mastering high-elbow freestyle with bilateral side-breathing and continuous backstroke.
* **Sales \& Outreach Script**:

> \\\\\\\*"This is the 1st of 4 stroke development levels where we transition from survival to technical swimming. This level serves as swim team prep, focusing on perfecting high-elbow freestyle and backstroke with proper rotary side-breathing techniques."\\\\\\\*

\---

### Shark 1

* **Target Age**: 3 to 12 years old (Stroke Development 2 — Breaststroke \& Butterfly Introduction)
* **Ratio**: 6:1
* **Level Color**: `#0099FF` (Sky Blue)
* **Core Goal**: Introducing breaststroke kick/pull timing and butterfly body dolphin wave while building 50-yard endurance.
* **Sales \& Outreach Script**:

> \\\\\\\*"This is the 2nd of 4 stroke development levels and continues swim team prep. Now that freestyle and backstroke are mastered, we introduce the breaststroke (pull-breathe-kick-glide) and butterfly (dolphin kick and arm recovery)."\\\\\\\*

\---

### Shark 2

* **Target Age**: 3 to 12 years old (Stroke Development 3 — 4-Stroke Mastery \& Competitive Turns)
* **Ratio**: 6:1
* **Level Color**: `#666699` (Slate Purple)
* **Core Goal**: Perfecting all 4 competitive strokes, flip turns, open turns, and streamlined competitive racing starts.
* **Sales \& Outreach Script**:

> \\\\\\\*"This is the 3rd of 4 stroke development levels and the final stage of swim team prep. We refine all four competitive strokes (Freestyle, Backstroke, Breaststroke, Butterfly) and introduce flip turns, open turns, and streamlined starts."\\\\\\\*

## 3\. Pre-Swim Team Track

### Barracuda 1, 2 \& 3

* **Target Age**: 5 to 17 years old (Competitive Swim Team Preparation \& Conditioning)
* **Ratio**: 6:1 to 8:1 (Team practice lane format)
* **Level Colors**:

  * Barracuda 1: `#003300` (Dark Forest Green)
  * Barracuda 2: `#006600` (Medium Green)
  * Barracuda 3: `#00A35C` (Emerald Green)
* **Core Goal**: Endurance conditioning, pace clock interval training, speed optimization, race starts, and natatorium/high school team readiness.
* **Sales \& Outreach Script**:

> \\\\\\\*"This is our pre-swim team program divided into three progressive tiers. It functions like a competitive swim team practice where we focus on advanced distance, speed, pace clock intervals, and IM transitions to prepare swimmers to try out for any club or school team."\\\\\\\*

## 4\. Teens \& Young Adults Track (Ages 13–17+)

### Young Adult 1

* **Level Color**: `#6699FF` | **Ratio**: 4:1
* **Focus**: Teen water acclimation, conquering fear/hesitation, and mastering independent back floating in a peer-comfortable environment.
* **Script**: *"Beginner survival level for teens. Focuses on water comfort, independent back floating, and core safety fundamentals."*

### Young Adult 2

* **Level Color**: `#0066FF` | **Ratio**: 4:1
* **Focus**: Freestyle with rotary side-breathing, technical backstroke, and continuous swimming efficiency.
* **Script**: *"Moves from survival basics into stroke mechanics, building continuous freestyle, backstroke, and aerobic stamina."*

### Young Adult 3

* **Level Color**: `#00205B` | **Ratio**: 4:1
* **Focus**: All 4 competitive strokes, flip turns, distance lap endurance, fitness, and junior lifeguard/swim team prep.
* **Script**: *"Advanced teen level refining all four strokes, starts, turns, and endurance for fitness or competitive teams."*

\---

## 5\. Adult Program Track (Ages 18+)

### Adult Level 1

* **Level Color**: `#00CCFF` | **Ratio**: 3:1
* **Focus**: Adult water acclimation, breath control, conquering fear, deep water comfort, and independent back floating.
* **Script**: *"Beginner survival level for adults. Focuses on conquering hesitation, breath control, and independent back floating in a supportive setting."*

### Adult Level 2

* **Level Color**: `#6699FF` | **Ratio**: 3:1
* **Focus**: Freestyle with rhythmic side-breathing, backstroke, breaststroke basics, and building lap endurance.
* **Script**: *"Transitions from survival into stroke development, focusing on continuous freestyle and backstroke for fitness and leisure."*

### Adult Level 3

* **Level Color**: `#0066FF` | **Ratio**: 3:1
* **Focus**: All 4 strokes, flip turns, distance training, open-water/triathlon preparation, and cardiovascular fitness.
* **Script**: *"Advanced adult swimming for stroke refinement, flip turns, endurance lap swimming, and triathlon training."*

## 6\. Adaptive \& Specialized Tracks

### Dolphin (Special Needs / Adaptive)

* **Target Audience**: Students of any age with sensory, physical, cognitive, or behavioral accommodations.
* **Format**: **1:1 Private** or **2:1 Semi-Private** (customized pace).
* **Level Color**: `#9999FF` (Lavender Blue)
* **Core Goal**: Individualized water safety, sensory regulation, and swim skill progression tailored to the student's unique needs and strengths.
* **Sales \& Outreach Script**:

> \\\\\\\*"This is our specialized program for swimmers with auxiliary or sensory needs. We focus on water acclimation and survival skills at the student's own pace. Offered as a private or semi-private lesson to ensure the highest safety and progress."\\\\\\\*
